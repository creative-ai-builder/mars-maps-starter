The Idea
READ THIS FIRST

When you talk to an AI, you write a prompt — a message that tells the AI exactly what to build or do.

The difference between a great prompt and a bad one isn't how long it is. It's how specific it is.

A good prompt has three parts:

  • Goal — what do you want to exist when it's done?
  • Context — what does the AI need to know about your situation?
  • Constraints — what rules must it follow? (color, size, position, exact wording)
❌ "Make the card look better"

✅ "Change the card background to dark navy (#1a1a2e), make the title 24px in white, and add a thin orange border (2px solid #e8691a)"

The second prompt tells the AI exactly what "better" means. It can only get it right because you were specific.
