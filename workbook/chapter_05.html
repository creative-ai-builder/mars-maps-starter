The Idea
READ THIS FIRST

By now you've built real things with AI. You know how to be specific, break things down, and catch mistakes. The last skill is the hardest one:

Knowing when to stop.

A director doesn't do everything they could do. They do what makes the demo land. They make the call.

Three questions a director asks before adding anything new:

  • Will this be visible in the demo? If parents won't see it, skip it.
  • Do I have time to test it? An untested feature is a risk, not an improvement.
  • Does it serve my Mission? If it doesn't help tell your story, cut it.
Good director call: "My app has a small alignment bug. I have 45 minutes. I'll fix the bug and write my demo script. No new features."

Scope creep: "My app works. Let me add an animated loading screen, a sound effect, and a dark mode." (No time to test any of it.)

The best demos aren't the most complex ones. They're the ones where the story is clear.
